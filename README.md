## 🌊 Ripple Theory

Ripple Theory begins with a simple claim:

Reality is not experienced raw.

Reality is rendered through layers.

Base reality is what is physically, materially, and practically happening.

Observer reality is what a person can perceive from where they stand, with what they know, feel, remember, fear, and expect.

Perception is the meaning the mind creates from an event.

Shared reality is the version of the world people build together through language, culture, repetition, conflict, silence, agreement, and social cues.

Trigger points are moments where an old pathway activates before conscious choice has fully caught up.

Intervention points are moments where a new action, sentence, person, choice, pause, boundary, or act of care enters the system and changes what can happen next.

Ripples are the way one action, mood, sentence, behavior, or visible cue can move through other people and change the room.

Architecture is the structure around an experience that makes some actions easier, some harder, some visible, some invisible, some rewarded, and some punished.

Feedback is what happens after the system responds, teaching the observer what seems possible next.
