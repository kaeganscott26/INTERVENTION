## 🧭 Non-negotiable rules

> The question leads. The mathematics investigates. Reality gets the final vote.

> Story is not proof. Science is not certainty. Belief is not model.

- Do not assume the desired result: no predetermined 3D, torus, or spatial topology.
- Mark every assumption, definition, derivation, empirical input, and speculative interpretation.
- Derive before assigning physical meaning; treat contradictions and null results as findings.
- Do not call a statistical metric a physical distance or a graph an observed spacetime.
- Distinguish mathematical consistency from evidence and established physical constraints.
- Do not retrofit free parameters to observations and call the result a prediction.
- Preserve failed constructions rather than quietly overwriting them.

## 🧾 Evidence classes

**Assumption:** input not derived here. **Definition:** mathematical specification. **Derived result:** logically follows from stated inputs. **Empirical input:** observation or tested physics constraining the model. **Speculative interpretation:** proposed physical meaning not demonstrated.
