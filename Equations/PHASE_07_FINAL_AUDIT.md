## 🧱 Assumptions register

1. Proposed unbounded common temporal parameter (T).
2. Finite classical internal real variables (X_i) and differentiability.
3. Chosen Lagrangian and potential, positive inertia/restoring constants.
4. Pairwise coupling variables for **every** unordered pair.
5. Symmetry (g_{ij}=g_{ji}) and equal coefficients to obtain permutation invariance.
6. Extra equilibrium ensemble assumption in 07.04.
7. Chosen mappings from coupling/correlation to candidate distances.
8. Quartic stabilization and double-well potentials in 07.07.
9. Initial conditions and finite-energy constraints for particular results.

None of these assumptions is an observed law merely because its consequences were derived.

## 🔍 Corrections and precision notes

- Statistical distance is a metric on equivalence classes of normalized random variables (a pseudometric on raw labels if distinct variables coincide almost surely).
- A bounded-below potential and conserved energy are separate conditions; the sufficient quartic bound is not a necessary condition.
- The independent double-well result is for the coupling sector, **not** a full stability theorem.
- Finite-graph analytic response does not exclude approximate emergent light-cone bounds in other systems.
- Phase 07 is *analytically complete as planned*, not computationally verified or experimentally confirmed.
