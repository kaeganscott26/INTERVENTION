## 🔎 Research status

**Mathematically constructed:** non-spatial parameterized dynamics; entropy over distinguishable states; coupled normal modes; a statistical metric; graph distances; dynamical couplings; bounded conservative toy models under stated conditions.

**Not established:** physical independence of time, origin of the action, emergent spatial locality, finite relativistic causal speed, physical distance, 3D space, gravity, or a novel parameter-free empirical prediction.

The Phase 07.07 models do **not** repair the original physical-emergence gap. In the simplest stable model the ground state has zero interactions; in an independent double-well coupling sector all pairs prefer nonzero strengths.
