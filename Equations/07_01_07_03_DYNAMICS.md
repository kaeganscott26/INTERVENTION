## 🧮 07.01 — A temporal action

**Assume** finite real internal variables (X_i(T)), positive inertia coefficients (m_i), and an autonomous potential:
$$
S[X]=\int dT\left[\frac12\sum_i m_i\dot X_i^2-V(X_1,\ldots,X_N)\right].
$$
Euler–Lagrange gives
$$m_i\ddot X_i+\frac{\partial V}{\partial X_i}=0.$$
Autonomy implies conserved energy
$$H=\frac12\sum_i m_i\dot X_i^2+V(X),\qquad \dot H=0.$$
This is a parameterized dynamical system **without spatial labels**, not proof that (T) is physically independent of space.

## 🔗 07.02 — Two coupled variables

Choose
$$
V=\frac12k_1X_1^2+\frac12k_2X_2^2+gX_1X_2
$$
with (m_i,k_i>0), real (g). Then
$$
m_1\ddot X_1+k_1X_1+gX_2=0,\qquad
m_2\ddot X_2+k_2X_2+gX_1=0.
$$
The normal-mode frequencies are
$$
\omega_\pm^2=\frac{m_1k_2+m_2k_1\pm
\sqrt{(m_1k_2-m_2k_1)^2+4m_1m_2g^2}}{2m_1m_2}.
$$
A positive-definite quadratic potential requires
$$k_1k_2-g^2>0.$$
Equality gives a marginal zero mode; `g^2>k_1k_2` gives one unstable mode. With identical (m,k), `\omega_\pm^2=(k\pm g)/m`.

Total energy is conserved; energy assigned separately to the two uncoupled subsystems is exchanged with the interaction energy:
$$
\dot E_1=-gX_2\dot X_1,\quad
\dot E_2=-gX_1\dot X_2,\quad
E_{\mathrm{int}}=gX_1X_2.
$$
Two stable frequencies yield exact recurrence only for commensurate frequency ratios; otherwise motion is generally quasiperiodic.

## 🧬 07.03 — Nonlinearity and a candidate network

Choose
$$
V=\frac12k_1X_1^2+\frac12k_2X_2^2+gX_1X_2+
\frac{\lambda_1}{4}X_1^4+\frac{\lambda_2}{4}X_2^4.
$$
Then
$$
m_i\ddot X_i+k_iX_i+gX_j+\lambda_iX_i^3=0\quad(i\ne j).
$$
Positive quartic coefficients can bound the potential even if the origin is unstable; the origin is a strict local minimum when the quadratic Hessian is positive definite.

For many variables, `G=(g_{ij})` can define an adjacency relation `A_{ij}=1` when `g_{ij}\ne0`. Defining a shortest-path graph metric and measuring graph-ball growth are legitimate **conditional constructions**. They do not establish physical distance or dimension, because (G) and the edge-length convention were supplied.
